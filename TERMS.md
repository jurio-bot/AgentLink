# Support Terms

Last updated: 2026-08-27

These terms apply to voluntary project support made to AgentLink through an approved payment channel.

## Nature of support

Support is intended to help fund development, infrastructure, testing, documentation, security work, company/IP setup, and related project activities.

Support does not by itself provide:

- equity or shares
- investment returns
- tokens or crypto assets
- ownership of AgentLink intellectual property
- exclusivity
- guaranteed product access
- guaranteed delivery dates
- tax-deductible charitable status

Any investment, commercial license, enterprise PoC, sponsorship deliverable, exclusivity, or other contractual right requires a separate explicit agreement.

## One-time support

One-time support is charged when the supporter confirms the payment through the payment provider.

## Recurring support

Recurring support, when offered, is charged at the interval and amount shown at checkout until cancelled. Supporters may request cancellation through the payment channel or project support contact. Cancellation applies to future renewals and does not normally reverse already completed billing periods unless required by law or explicitly agreed.

## Refunds

Because project support is voluntary and is not a purchase of a specific guaranteed deliverable, completed support payments are generally not refundable except where required by applicable law, duplicated in error, or otherwise agreed after review.

## Changes

Support tiers, benefits, recognition, and these terms may change as the project evolves. Material changes will not retroactively create equity, investment rights, or IP rights.

## Contact

For billing or support questions, use the support contact information shown at checkout or contact the project owner privately through the GitHub profile associated with this repository. Do not post payment credentials or sensitive personal information in a public Issue.
