# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-27

AgentLink is an early-stage project developing persistent, permissioned, recoverable execution infrastructure for AI workers.

## Information we may receive

Depending on how you interact with the project, we may receive limited information such as:

- contact information you voluntarily provide
- support or sponsorship inquiry details
- GitHub account information visible through GitHub interactions
- payment and billing information processed by our payment provider
- technical information necessary to operate and secure public project services

## Payment processing

Payments, when enabled, are processed by Stripe. AgentLink does not intentionally store full payment-card numbers in this public repository or in ordinary project documentation.

Stripe may collect and process information under its own privacy terms in order to process payments, prevent fraud, satisfy legal obligations, and provide receipts or billing services.

## How information may be used

Information may be used to:

- process project support or sponsorship
- respond to support, partnership, or investment inquiries
- provide receipts or billing-related communication
- prevent abuse, fraud, or security incidents
- comply with legal and accounting obligations
- improve project documentation, onboarding, and operations

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers such as payment processors, hosting providers, or professional advisers when reasonably necessary for the purposes above or to comply with law.

## Retention

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for operational, accounting, security, contractual, or legal purposes.

## Security

Reasonable care is taken to keep private information out of public repositories and public Issues. Please do not post credentials, payment details, private addresses, customer data, or other sensitive information in public GitHub Issues.

## Contact

For privacy-related requests, contact the project owner privately through the GitHub profile associated with this repository. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public Issue.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the project, company structure, and payment infrastructure evolve.
