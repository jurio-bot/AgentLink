# Support AgentLink

AgentLink is an early-stage project building persistent, permissioned, recoverable execution infrastructure for AI workers.

We welcome support from individuals, patrons, companies, cloud providers, research partners, and other organizations that want to help the project move faster without requiring the AgentLink core IP to be sold or opened.

### Corporate sponsors

We are open to sponsorship from companies that want to support reliable AI-agent infrastructure.

Possible forms include direct project sponsorship, cloud / GPU / API credits, hardware or test-device support, engineering or security review support, event / demo sponsorship, research collaboration, and enterprise design-partner PoCs.

Corporate support does not automatically grant ownership of AgentLink core IP, exclusivity, or control over the public roadmap. Any such rights require a separate explicit agreement.

### Strategic patrons

We are also interested in longer-term patrons who want to support the AgentLink mission while allowing the project to remain technically independent and model-agnostic.

A strategic patron may support infrastructure, R&D, commercialization, international expansion, security work, or specific public technical demonstrations.

### In-kind support

Useful non-cash support can include cloud credits, GPU / compute capacity, development hardware, test devices, security review, legal / IP guidance, enterprise sandbox access, and introductions to design partners or investors.

## What supporters receive

Supporter benefits depend on the support type and any applicable agreement. Potential non-equity recognition can include public acknowledgment, supporter / sponsor listing, early demo briefings, technical update summaries, and participation in selected public or design-partner discussions.

No equity, token, investment return, tax deduction, product guarantee, exclusivity, or IP right is implied by informal support or public recognition.

## Important legal / accounting note

AgentLink is currently being structured as a project and future company. This support program is **not** presented as a tax-deductible charitable donation program.

Financial support is accepted through documented payment channels with appropriate legal and accounting treatment. Corporate sponsorships involving deliverables, commercial rights, exclusivity, or procurement should use a separate written agreement rather than an informal patron tier.
