## Pricing rule

- Low price is not the product. Each SKU must include a finished output, verification, or operational handoff that a bare prompt does not provide.
- A customer who already pays for capable AI must not be sold prompt-only output.
- Higher prices require real files, devices, code, environments, integrations, recovery, testing, or meaningful production work.
- Large / ambiguous jobs are quote-only after real scope is visible.
- No fabricated users, reviews, adoption numbers, revenue, or performance guarantees.
